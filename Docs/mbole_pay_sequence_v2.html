Mbole Pay — Sequence Diagrams

Two essential event flows showing actor ↔ system ↔ external service interactions  ·  June 2026

Sequence 1 — Contribution Payment & Automatic Payout

A member initiates a contribution payment. The system creates a Payment record, calls Fapshi to push a USSD request to the member's phone, then processes either the webhook confirmation or falls back to polling. Once all members in the cycle have paid, the payout is automatically triggered to the current rotation recipient via Fapshi transfer. Failed payouts are held in escrow.

Sequence 2 — Dispute Filing & Resolution

A group member files a dispute, optionally uploading evidence to IPFS via Pinata. The system opens a 72-hour peer voting window and notifies all group members. The scheduler auto-resolves based on vote majority at deadline. An admin can force-resolve at any point.
