Mbole Pay — Activity Diagrams

Three core business flows  ·  as-built June 2026

Payment Contribution & Payout Flow

Covers: member initiates a contribution payment → Fapshi USSD push → webhook confirmation → retry logic on failure → delinquency marking → payout trigger when all contributions are paid → Fapshi transfer to recipient → escrow on exhausted retries.

Dispute Filing & Resolution Flow

Covers: member files a dispute with evidence → evidence stored on IPFS via Pinata → 72-hour voting window → peer voting → scheduler auto-resolution at deadline → admin force-resolve path.
